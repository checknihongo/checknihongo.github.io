Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

This app requires no account, sign-in, location or contacts; your study history stays on your device.

Video links. When you paste a YouTube link in the Video tab, the app requests that link from YouTube to fetch its title. No other user data leaves the device.

Advertising. The Study, Stats, Video and Words tabs show Google AdMob banner ads. To serve them, Google may collect and process device identifiers and advertising-related usage data. On first launch the app asks for App Tracking Transparency consent. If you allow it, ads may match your interests; if you decline or don't answer, the app requests non-personalized ads only. Either way, every feature works the same. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
